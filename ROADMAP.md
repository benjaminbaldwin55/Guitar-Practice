# Roadmap: beginner to advanced

The goal: one app Ben and Anna use for years, from the first chord to
playing solos and writing songs. Every stage adds lessons *and* the tools,
measurements and achievements that go with them, so practice always has a
next milestone a session or two away.

Rough timelines assume 20 to 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week. They will
stretch or shrink; the app follows the measurements, not the calendar.

## The lead guitar track (starts now, runs through every stage)

Soloing isn't saved for later. It runs alongside chords from day one,
because Ben already knows box 1 and lead playing is his north star. It
becomes its own block type in the session builder, rotating with strum,
scale and progression blocks.

1. **Box 1 and phrasing** (now): A minor pentatonic box 1, then making
   *phrases* instead of running the scale: 3 and 4 note ideas, rests,
   repeating a phrase, ending on the root. Jam over backing tracks.
2. **Licks library**: short licks, each measured with a clean-tempo ladder
   like chord changes. Blues curl, Cantrell-style bends, Brothers Osborne
   country licks later.
3. **Technique, measured by the tuner's pitch detection**: bends that land
   on pitch (the app checks the target note), vibrato, slides,
   hammer-ons and pull-offs.
4. **Call and response trainer**: the app plays a short phrase, you play
   it back, pitch detection checks the notes. Ear training and soloing in
   one. Doubles as a together game (Ben leads, Anna answers, or swap).
5. **Boxes 2 to 5 and connecting them**, then the E minor and major
   pentatonic for country and Stay a Little Longer.
6. **Solos over changes**: target chord tones over a 12-bar blues, then
   learning real solos note for note, slowed down and looped.

Achievements for this track: First lick, 5 / 10 / 25 licks, In-tune bend
(three bends within 10 cents), Echo (10 call-and-response phrases right),
Box 2, All five boxes, First solo over a 12-bar, Licks at 120 BPM,
Stay a Little Longer solo.

## Stage 1: Foundations (months 0 to 3) ← we are here
- Open chords: Em, Am, E, A, C, G, D, Dm ✅
- Chord-change path with unlocks, counted by mic ✅
- Strumming patterns, eighth-note feel ✅
- A minor and E minor pentatonic shapes ✅
- Tuner, metronome, session builder, achievements ✅
- **Next:** Songs with milestone ladders (first full song), progress charts,
  Anna's profile and pacing

## Stage 2: Songs and rhythm (months 3 to 9)
- Power chords, palm muting, accents, the DS-1 grunge sound
- Syncopated and 16th-note strumming; the drummer advantage
- Capo and transposing; Drop D and half-step-down songs
- Reading tab, simple riffs (Come As You Are, Rooster)
- Anna: singing while strumming, starting with two-chord songs
- **Tools:** drum machine and backing tracks, recordings with
  then-vs-now playback, song section looping and slow-down
- **Achievements:** first full song, five songs, first riff, first duet

## Stage 3: Barre chords and lead basics (months 6 to 15)
- F and Bm barre chords, then the E and A shapes anywhere on the neck
- All five pentatonic boxes, connecting them
- Bends, vibrato, slides, hammer-ons and pull-offs, each with a clean-tempo
  ladder like the chord changes
- A licks library, starting with Jerry Cantrell and blues staples
- Lead over a 12-bar blues
- **Tools:** fretboard note trainer, bend pitch checker (the tuner already
  knows how), lick recorder
- **Achievements:** Barre none, all five boxes, first solo

## Stage 4: Intermediate (years 1 to 2)
- Stay a Little Longer, solo included (Ben's big goal)
- Major pentatonic and country licks, hybrid picking, double stops
- Triads and CAGED, so chords and solos connect
- Ear training: intervals, chord quality, figuring out riffs by ear
- Writing parts for two guitars plus Anna's vocals
- **Tools:** ear trainer, transcription helper (slow and loop any
  recording), weekly coach review
- **Achievements:** By ear, duo arrangement, first transcription

## Stage 5: Advanced (years 2 and beyond)
- Modes in context, arpeggios, playing over chord changes
- Speed building with long-term tempo charts
- Fingerstyle and travis picking (Shelf in the Room territory)
- Songwriting: progressions, structure, recording demos
- Playing out: open mics, a set list for the two of you
- **Tools:** set list mode, practice planner across weeks, recording
  projects
- **Achievements:** first original song, first gig, 1,000 hours

## Achievement ideas backlog
Add these as the features that measure them arrive.
- Songs: first full song, 5 / 10 / 25 songs, a song in a new tuning,
  a song with a capo, playing a full song at the original tempo
- Recordings: first recording, "Then and now" (two takes of the same thing
  30 days apart), 10 recordings
- Ear training: 10 / 50 / 100 intervals right, a 7-day ear streak, tune
  the guitar by ear (the tuner confirms afterward)
- Together: first duet, Anna sings while playing a whole song, a set list
  of 3 / 5 songs, 10 call-and-response rounds
- Rhythm: steady strumming against the metronome (measured by mic timing),
  a 16th-note pattern at 80 BPM, a full 12-bar without stopping
- Gear and tone: play with the looper, try all four metronome sounds,
  save a tone preset for a song
- Comebacks: practice again after a week off ("Back at it"), a goal week
  after a missed one. Rewards returning, never punishes breaks
