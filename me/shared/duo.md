# Ben + Anna

## How we play together
- Purely cooperative. Shared goals, never competing.
- **Together is ad hoc**, not the default. Practicing side by side can feel
  awkward. Most practice is solo; together sessions happen when they want.
- **Best together time (Anna):** learning a new concept that challenges
  both of them, so they can bounce ideas off each other. Together sessions
  should center on something new to both, not repeat solo drills.
- One device (the laptop) runs the session for both.

## Natural duo setup
- Ben on rhythm, Anna singing
- Then: Anna on a simplified part, Ben on the full part
- Then: swap
- Lead and response: Ben plays a pentatonic phrase, Anna answers by ear (her
  strength), or the other way round

## Duo song ideas

- Yellow: Coldplay (on Anna's list; alt-rock enough for Ben)
